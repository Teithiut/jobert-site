# Title

## Life Update

Since I didn't get the chance to share it in last week's post, I went to watch the fireworks downtown after work on the **31st**. The lack of audible backing music (there was only one place it was being broadcast from, and I wasn't sitting near it) made the experience far worse than previous years', even though the fireworks display itself was of comparable quality. Since the fireworks were funded by the city this year rather than by a corporate sponsor like previous times, this entire experience made me feel like I was watching my tax dollars being incinerated right in front of my eyes (which is technically accurate, but still...). At least I managed to catch an Early Music Vancouver ad at a bus stop on the way home.
