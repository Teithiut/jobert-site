## Life Update

After working for three weeks straight for Early Music Vancouver, one of the first things I did upon no longer being employed was to create an invoice for services rendered on the **7th**. I already had notes of how long I worked for and what I did, but the most complicated part was creating the invoice itself, since the templates I found weren't to my liking and didn't support the table formulae I wanted to use to make payment calculations easier. As a result, I spent the better part of two hours making my own template from scratch based on what I saw online, and I'm happy to report that it makes my life easier the way I intended it to. Hopefully, I'll have more chances to use it in the future so my hard work doesn't go to waste.
