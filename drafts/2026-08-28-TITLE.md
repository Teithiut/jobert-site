## Cinematheque

In this section, I'll be discussing films I watched lately.

As teased last week, here's my thoughts on the film **Remarkably Bright Creatures**, directed by Olivia Newman and starring Sally Field, Lewis Pullman, and Alfred Molina. The film centres around Tova (Field), a woman near retirement who works as a small-town aquarium janitor trying to navigate life after the death of her son. When struggling wannabe musician Cameron (Pullman) ends up in town following an accident, the two of them begin gradually bonding over the loss of their family members, aided by the aquarium's octopus Marcellus.
